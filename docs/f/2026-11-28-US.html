Why this forecast

Anchor: loglinear (damped exponential) had the lowest backtest MAE at every horizon (h3 MAE 1503 vs 3344-4641 for the others) and its bias-corrected medians (21553/30291/37320/52159) sit at the top of the method spread, consistent with the recent trajectory: admissions have risen 13 consecutive weeks, +13% wk/wk last week after +19% and +16%, and ILI is still climbing (3.80% and rising). I trimmed the loglinear medians slightly (20000/25000/30000/38000) because growth is decelerating (13% < 19% < 16% pattern suggests the peak is near) and the last observed week (13668) is provisional and likely to revise upward, so h0 should sit above it. The hub ensemble had not submitted, so no external prior to blend; the flat baseline (13668) is clearly too low given the trend. Intervals: I used lognormal shapes with sigma widening by horizon (0.16/0.21/0.23/0.25), wider than the backtest-implied intervals because the backtest assumes the recent past repeats, and this is a fast-rising season approaching a possible peak/inflection where flu can turn faster than it looks; the right tail is longer than the left to reflect right-skew and the risk of a sharper surge. What would change my mind: if next week's observed count is below ~17000, the peak is likely already here and I would shift mass down; if it exceeds ~25000, the season is accelerating and I would shift up.
