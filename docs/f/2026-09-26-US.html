Why this forecast

Anchor: the gbt model (lowest backtest MAE at h3, 335.5) and the cross-method consensus (h1=3218, h2=4074, h3=6791). The hub had no submission for this week (off-season; last submissions were in May), so there was no ensemble to beat. I moved slightly above the consensus medians because the recent trajectory is a fast, early start: admissions have grown +23% to +35% wk/wk over the last four weeks (1241 on 9/5 to 2515 on 9/26), and ILI is also climbing (1.36% to 1.63%), consistent with an early, strong 2026-27 season. I set h0=2600 (the observed 2515 is provisional and likely to revise up), h1=3200, h2=4100, and h3=6800 (leaning toward the upper gbt value of 7311 given the early strong start). Uncertainty is widened with horizon and made right-skewed: the statistical methods disagree by 1.82x at h3, and the season is near an inflection where flu can turn faster than it looks, so the h3 interval is deliberately wide (3384-13663). I would revise down if ILI flattens over the next two weeks, and up if the weekly growth rate holds above ~30%.
