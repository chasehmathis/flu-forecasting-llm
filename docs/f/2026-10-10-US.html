Why this forecast

Anchor: the FluSight hub ensemble (medians 3959/4490/5580/6480 for h0-h3), cross-checked against the statistical model comparison where gbt had the best backtest MAE (556 at h3) and medians ran 4476/6357/10997/15961. The last observed week (3246, +24% wk/wk) is provisional and a floor, and ILI is still rising (1.85% and climbing), so I moved medians modestly above the ensemble: 4100/5100/6400/7900, i.e. roughly +4% at h0 to +22% at h3, tracking the observed ~24-27% weekly growth with mild deceleration as the season matures. I did not go as high as gbt/loglinear because the ensemble's slower implied growth is a reasonable prior for the deceleration that typically follows the initial exponential phase, and the methods disagree by up to 2.4x at h3. Intervals are lognormal-shaped, right-skewed, and widened with horizon (95% spans ~2.4x at h0 to ~2.6x at h3, with 97.5% bounds reaching 6839/9782/14114/20031) to cover the method disagreement, the early-season inflection risk, and upward revision of recent weeks.
