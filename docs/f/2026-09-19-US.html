Why this forecast

Anchor: backtest-weighted statistical models. GBT had the lowest h3 MAE (218) and seasonal the lowest h1/h2; I blended their medians (h1 2450, h2 3050, h3 3900), pulling h3 down from GBT's 5303 because the 2024-25 analogue (a mild season with a low September base) suggests the current early rise is more like a normal-season start than a severe one. No hub ensemble exists for this week (off-season), so no external prior to beat. Adjustments: ILINet %ILI is rising (0.87→1.58 over 5 weeks) and admissions have grown ~25%/wk for 4 weeks, confirming a genuine early-season start rather than noise, so I kept the upward trajectory rather than flattening it. The last observed week (1964) is provisional and likely to revise upward, so h0 median sits slightly above it (2000) with a wide interval. Intervals widen with horizon and are right-skewed (lognormal with larger upper sigma) to cover the possibility of a faster takeoff: h3 spans 1688-11108, covering both a stall and a 2023-24-style early surge. What would change my mind: a flattening of %ILI or admissions next week would argue for a lower h2/h3; a jump to >3000 in the next observed week would argue for moving toward the GBT upper tail.
