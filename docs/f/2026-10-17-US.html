Why this forecast

Anchor: the statistical-model consensus (h0 5348, h1 8117, h2 13225, h3 19112) vs the FluSight hub ensemble (h0 4831, h1 5679, h2 6785, h3 7863). The last observed week (2026-10-10) is 3835 and provisional, so it is a floor, not a centre. I split the gap between the two anchors but leaned toward the statistical models because the season is starting early and rising unusually fast: ILI has climbed from 0.87% (Aug 8) to 2.07% (Oct 10), and Truveta reports late-September influenza-associated hospitalization rates ~3x higher than the same point in the prior six seasons, with a rise 2.5x faster. That early/fast start supports the higher seasonal/gbt/loglinear estimates over the hub's flatter trajectory. Medians: h0 5050, h1 6800, h2 9150, h3 11800. Intervals are right-skewed (upper tail ~1.9-2.5x the median, lower tail ~0.5-0.6x) and widen with horizon (sigma 0.30 -> 0.54), reflecting the large method disagreement at h2/h3 (up to 2.2x spread), the inflection risk as flu accelerates, and the fact that recent weeks revise upward as late reports arrive.
